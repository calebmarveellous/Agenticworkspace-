# Ideal Client Profiles (ICP) — Global, Remote-First, High-Ticket

Rule: an ICP is only usable if you can name where to find 50+ of them this week. Every profile below includes sourcing channels.

## ICP 1 — DTC E-commerce Brands ($500K–$10M/yr revenue), US/UK/EU/AU
**Pain:** Rising Meta/Google CPMs, flat ROAS, no clear media-buying ownership, founder doing ads themselves at 2am.
**Why you win:** Direct experience with cosmetics/accessories brands (Luscents, McDobi GLOBAL, Hopmichar) is a 1:1 vertical match. You can speak their exact language: AOV, ROAS, LTV:CAC, retargeting funnels.
**Offer angle:** Hormozi-style guarantee — "I find and fix the leak in your funnel in 14 days or you don't pay the retainer."
**Where to find them:** Shopify app store review threads, Facebook Ads Library (search competitor brands, see who's actively spending), TikTok Shop sellers, Instagram accounts with 10K-100K followers and visible product tags, BeenVerified/Klaviyo partner directories.
**Ticket size:** $1,500–$5,000/mo retainer or 10-15% of ad spend managed.

## ICP 2 — FMCG / CPG Challenger Brands (regional or national, pre-Series A)
**Pain:** Needs both brand-building and performance marketing; usually has zero in-house paid media competency.
**Why you win:** Suncious Foods experience proves you can operate in low-margin, high-volume categories where CAC discipline is everything.
**Where to find them:** LinkedIn Sales Navigator filtered by industry (Food & Beverage) + company size 11-50 + job titles "Founder/CEO/Head of Marketing," trade association directories, Expo West / regional trade show exhibitor lists.
**Ticket size:** $2,000–$6,000/mo.

## ICP 3 — B2B Marketplaces & Agri-Tech / Trade Platforms
**Pain:** Needs lead-gen funnels and data-driven growth, not just "brand awareness" — B2B buyers convert on trust and data.
**Why you win:** Zonia Hub (agricultural B2B marketplace) experience is a rare, specific credential almost no competing freelancer/agency has.
**Where to find them:** LinkedIn (search "agri-tech," "B2B marketplace," "supply chain platform" + Series Seed/A funding via Crunchbase), AgFunder News advertiser lists, industry Slack/Discord communities.
**Ticket size:** $2,500–$8,000/mo (B2B retainers command premium pricing due to higher deal values downstream).

## ICP 4 — Fintech / Trading Education & Signal Services
**Pain:** High customer acquisition cost, compliance-sensitive ad creative, needs a media buyer who understands performance + risk.
**Why you win:** James Trading Strategies experience is direct proof of competency in a notoriously hard-to-advertise niche.
**Where to find them:** Trading Discord/Telegram communities (as a member, not a spammer — build authority first), Twitter/X fintech creator space, affiliate networks for trading education products.
**Ticket size:** $3,000–$10,000/mo or performance-based (% of funded accounts/signups).

## ICP 5 — Global Coaches, Consultants & Info-Product Sellers (value-ladder ready)
**Pain:** Has an offer but no funnel; wants a Hormozi/Suby-style value ladder built and run.
**Why you win:** This is literally your stated methodology — market it as a productized offer ("Value Ladder Build + 90-Day Media Buy").
**Where to find them:** Podcast guest lists in the coaching/consulting space, ClickFunnels/Kajabi "built with" footers, YouTube ad library for course creators actively running ads (signals budget).
**Ticket size:** $2,000–$7,000/mo + optional rev-share.

## Prioritization for the next 25 days
Rank order by speed-to-close: **ICP 1 (DTC e-comm) → ICP 5 (coaches/info-product) → ICP 2 (FMCG) → ICP 3 (B2B) → ICP 4 (fintech)**. DTC and info-product sellers have the shortest sales cycles and clearest ROI story — front-load outreach volume there in Week 1, layer in ICP 2-4 from Week 2 once your sequences are proven.
