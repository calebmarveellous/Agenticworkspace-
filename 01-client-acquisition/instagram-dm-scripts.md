# Instagram DM Scripts — Manual Send Only

**Read this first:** these are for YOU to paste and send yourself, from your own Instagram account, one at a time, to a real person. Never automate Instagram DM sending — bulk/automated messaging violates Meta's Platform Policy and Instagram's Terms of Use, and gets accounts rate-limited, shadowbanned, or permanently banned. A human sending a personalized DM to an account they actually looked at is fine; a bot doing it at volume is exactly what their spam detection is built to catch.

**Format rules for DMs (different from email):** shorter than the email version — 2-4 sentences max. No numbered value list (reads as spammy in a DM). One soft CTA. Same standing rule as everything else: lead with value/interest, not a name-drop, and never claim a stat you can't back up.

**Sequence:** send the opener. If no reply in 3-4 days, one soft follow-up ("still happy to send that teardown if useful!") — then stop. Don't chase past two touches on DM; it reads as pushy in a way email doesn't.

## Before sending ANY of these
1. Confirm the handle is still correct and active (accounts change).
2. Skim their last 5-10 posts for anything you can reference specifically — a real detail beats the generic opener every time.
3. Never send the same script to two prospects back to back without changing at least the opening line — Instagram's spam detection also flags near-identical repeated DMs from one account.
