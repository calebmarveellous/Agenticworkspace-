# Cold Outreach Templates — Direct-Response, ICP-Matched

Rules for every send: (1) personalize the [BRACKET] fields with real research, never leave a bracket unfilled, (2) one CTA per email, (3) no more than 90 seconds to read, (4) subject line under 6 words, (5) never claim a stat you can't back up.

---

## SEQUENCE A — ICP 1: DTC E-commerce Brands

**Email 1 (Day 0) — Subject: "[Brand]'s ROAS leak"**
```
Hey [First Name],

Spent 5 minutes on [Brand]'s Meta ad account activity (Ads Library) — noticed you're running [X creative angle] but not retargeting cart abandoners with a dedicated sequence.

That's usually a 15-30% recoverable-revenue gap.

I fixed the same leak for a cosmetics brand (Luscents) — took their retargeting ROAS from [X] to [Y] in [Z] days.

Worth a 15-min teardown of your funnel? No pitch, just the 3 fixes I'd make first. Free.

[Your Name]
```

**Email 2 (Day 3, if no reply) — Subject: "quick follow-up"**
```
[First Name] — following up in case this got buried.

If Q4/holiday ad spend is on your radar, the retargeting fix above compounds fast this time of year. Happy to send the teardown even if a call isn't a fit right now — just reply "SEND IT."

[Your Name]
```

**Email 3 (Day 7, breakup) — Subject: "closing the loop"**
```
[First Name] — I'll stop here unless you want the audit. If ad performance becomes a priority later, I'm one reply away.

[Your Name]
```
