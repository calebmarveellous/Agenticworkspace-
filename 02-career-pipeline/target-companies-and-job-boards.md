# Target Companies & Job Boards — Remote Growth/Media Buying Roles

**STANDING RULE (locked):** only apply to roles that are genuinely open to candidates anywhere in the world — not "remote" as a euphemism for "remote, but must be based in/authorized to work in [country/region]." A "Remote" tag on a job board is not enough by itself; verify the actual JD for country/region/timezone restrictions before adding a role to the application queue. Region-locked roles (e.g., "Remote - USA," "Remote, Asia," "Growth Manager - Vietnam," "Marketing Manager - LATAM") are excluded even if tagged "Remote," unless the JD explicitly states open-worldwide eligibility (timezone-alignment asks, like "must overlap EST hours," are fine — that's not a location restriction, just scheduling).

## How to reverse-engineer hiring criteria from a JD (do this for every application)
1. **Extract the exact keywords** used for skills/tools (e.g., "full-funnel," "MER," "incrementality testing") — these must appear verbatim in your tailored resume's Skills section.
2. **Identify the implied pain point.** JDs rarely state problems directly, but you can infer: "own paid social strategy end-to-end" = they likely have no dedicated media buyer yet = your pitch is "I can own this from day 1, here's proof." "Scale from $X to $Y ad spend" = they need someone who's handled budget scaling, not just small-budget management.
3. **Check the company's current ad activity** (Meta Ads Library, SimilarWeb, BuiltWith) before the interview — walk in with a specific observation, not generic prep.
4. **Note seniority signals** ("5+ years," "own strategy," vs. "support the team," "execute campaigns") — match your application tone/title accordingly; don't apply to senior/lead roles with a junior-toned resume or vice versa.
