# Global Remote Job Market Benchmarks — Growth Marketing / Media Buying (2026)

All figures below are pulled from live 2026 sources (Ziprecruiter, Glassdoor, Salary.com, Robert Half, Indeed, Payscale, Growth.Talent). Ranges vary by source methodology — treat these as negotiation anchors, not guarantees. Cross-check against the specific company's stage and geography before an offer conversation.

## Growth Marketing Manager (remote, US-listed roles)

| Source | Range/Average |
|---|---|
| ZipRecruiter (Jul 2026) | ~$83,488/yr average |
| Glassdoor | ~$130,449/yr average |
| Salary.com | ~$119,945/yr average |
| Robert Half 2026 Salary Guide | $91,250 – $133,250 |
| High-growth startup / senior IC (SF/NYC-anchored but remote-friendly) | $100K–$180K base + 10–20% bonus; $200K+ total comp at senior level |
| Overall market span (Growth.Talent guide) | $90K–$240K depending on seniority/company stage |

**Read:** Entry-to-mid remote growth roles cluster around $85K–$120K. Senior/lead roles at funded startups go $150K–$220K+. Your realistic 25-day target band, given a strong but early-career-stage portfolio: **$70K–$110K** for a first global remote placement, with upside to negotiate higher using hard ROAS/CAC numbers.

## Media Buyer / Paid Ads Specialist (remote, US-listed roles)

| Source | Range/Average |
|---|---|
| ZipRecruiter Remote Media Buyer (Jul 2026) | Avg ~$69,423/yr; typical range $55,000–$84,500 |
| Glassdoor Remote Media Buyer | ~$60,000/yr average |
| ZipRecruiter Remote Paid Media Specialist (Aug 2026) | Avg ~$63,789/yr; typical range $43,000–$72,500 |
| International remote-first employers (monthly) | $4,300–$5,500/mo typical; up to $15,400/mo at top end for senior specialists |

**Read:** Realistic 25-day target band for Media Buyer/Paid Ads roles: **$55K–$80K** to start, with contract/freelance-adjacent remote roles sometimes paying per-project at higher effective rates. If a role explicitly wants "full-funnel performance marketing + media buying + analytics" combined, price toward the top of the Growth Marketing band instead — that's a growth marketer job with a media-buyer title.

## Where the money actually is: company stage matters more than title

- **Bootstrapped/small agency clients:** lower base, but often open to contractor/fractional arrangements — useful for cash flow while the FT search runs.
- **Series A–C funded startups:** best blend of remote-friendliness + upside + willingness to pay for proven ROAS.
- **Global e-commerce/DTC brands:** highest volume of Media Buyer/Paid Social openings, most willing to hire outside the US/UK/EU if the portfolio proves performance in comparable verticals (cosmetics/FMCG experience — i.e., Luscents, Suncious Foods — is directly relevant here).
