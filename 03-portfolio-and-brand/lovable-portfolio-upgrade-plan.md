# Lovable Portfolio Upgrade Plan

Brand constraint (never violate): minimalist, Apple-inspired, high-contrast 3D, dark purple + dark gold + pure black palette. Every recommendation below fits within that system — no deviation into bright colors, cluttered layouts, or generic "marketing agency" templates.

## Section-by-section rebuild

### 1. Hero (above the fold)
- One sentence, direct-response, not a job title: e.g., "I turn ad spend into ROAS — for DTC, FMCG, and fintech brands, globally remote." Not "Digital Marketing Strategist | Social Media Manager | Media Buyer" (that's four identities, pick one framing for the primary hero and let the rest live in the About section).
- Primary CTA button: "Book a Free Funnel Audit" (dark-gold accent button, high contrast against black background) — links straight to Calendly.
- Secondary, smaller link: "See the case studies" — anchor-scrolls to proof section.

### 2. Proof bar (immediately below hero)
- 3-4 hard numbers in large type, minimal labels: e.g., "3.2x ROAS · 22K followers in 90 days · 38% lower CAC · 5 verticals served." Pull these from the metric checklist in `resume-ats-optimized.md` once you've extracted real numbers from ad accounts.
- This is the single highest-leverage addition — buyers and recruiters both scan for numbers before reading paragraphs.

### 3. Case studies (one per client, structured identically for scannability)
For each of Zonia Hub, Suncious Foods, Luscents, McDobi GLOBAL, Hopmichar, James Trading Strategies:
- **Challenge** (1 sentence) → **What I did** (2-3 bullets, tools named) → **Result** (numbers, large type) → optional before/after screenshot (ad creative or dashboard, blurred/redacted where confidential).
- Use consistent 3D card components (dark purple card on black background, gold number highlights) — this is where Lovable's component system should be leaned on for consistency, not one-off custom layouts per case study.

### 4. Services / offer section
- Structure as a value ladder, matching your own stated methodology: (1) Free Funnel Audit → (2) Growth Sprint (fixed-price, defined deliverable, e.g., "90-Day Paid Media Overhaul") → (3) Ongoing Retainer / Fractional Growth Partner.
- Price anchoring: show a "starting at" range per tier so unqualified leads self-select out before booking a call (saves you time across 1,000-client-scale outreach).

### 5. Tools/stack strip
- Logo row: Meta Ads Manager, Google Ads, TikTok Ads, GA4, HubSpot, Shopify, Unbounce, ClickFunnels — signals technical credibility fast, especially to recruiters screening for platform experience.

### 6. Testimonials / social proof
- Request a short written or video testimonial from at least 2-3 past clients this week (tie into the referral-activation outreach in `01-client-acquisition/cold-outreach-email-templates.md` — you're already messaging them).
- If testimonials aren't available yet, use specific attributed results instead ("Result verified via [Client]'s Meta Ads Manager account, [date range]") rather than generic unattributed praise.

### 7. About section (where the "jack of all trades" identity lives)
- This is the ONLY place the full range (Strategist, Social Media Manager, Media Buyer, Data Analyst) should appear — as depth/versatility context, after the hero has already anchored one primary positioning.
- Include certifications with logos (Simplilearn, LinkedIn, Udemy, Programming Hub) plus a visible "in progress: Meta Blueprint, Google Ads Certification" line — shows active credential-building.

### 8. Footer CTA (repeat the ask)
- Same primary CTA as hero. Add a secondary contact path (email/LinkedIn) for recruiters who prefer async contact over booking a call.

## Technical/SEO checklist
- Page load speed: compress all 3D renders/images (target <2s load — buyers and recruiters bounce fast).
- Meta title/description tailored for search: "Caleb Marvel — Growth Marketer & Media Buyer | Remote, Global" — helps if recruiters Google your name pre-interview.
- Mobile responsiveness check — a large % of LinkedIn-sourced traffic to your portfolio will be mobile.

## Priority order given the 25-day clock
1. Proof bar with real numbers (Day 1-2 — this alone changes conversion on every other channel).
2. Hero rewrite (Day 2).
3. Case study section (Day 3-5, one per day is fine).
4. Services/value-ladder section (Day 5-6).
5. Testimonials as they come in (ongoing).
Don't let portfolio polish delay outreach — ship the proof-bar and hero fix first, then keep improving in parallel with sending emails, not before.
